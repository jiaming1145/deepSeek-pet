- Cells: the 18 VRM presets (`neutral blink blinkLeft blinkRight aa ih ou ee oh happy angry sad
  relaxed surprised lookUp lookDown lookLeft lookRight`) + 12 customs (`sleepy affection panic shy
  smug pouty focused hurt confused shocked gentle cheerful`). Every cell = base skin + brows + eyes +
  mouth + fx blitted with the kit's anchors (same maths as `verify_atlas.py`), flattened onto the skin
  colour (alpha 255 everywhere: the face material is OPAQUE), downscaled once with Lanczos and

## 2. build_vrm.py on the stock rig; the head defect (verified)

Builds run (`--face-material "^eye$"`, chains, `--auto-morphs`, exit 0 each):
- stock atlas: `--face-texture out/stock/face_atlas.png --face-atlas out/stock/face_atlas.json --outline-skip "^body_bake$"` -> `out/build/stock_character.vrm`, 18 presets.
- Whale-chan face: `--face-texture spikes/model/face/face_texture.png --face-atlas spikes/model/face/face_atlas_states.json --outline-skip "^body_bake$"` -> `out/build/stock_whalechan_face.vrm`, 18 presets + 12 customs, 29 textureTransform binds on `eye`, blink/blinkLeft/blinkRight also carry the stock morph binds.

`eye` is the face-parts material of the stock head (eyes, mouth interior, brows; 1330 tris; UVs 0..1 of
`faceparts`). `body_bake` is the skin but is shared with the `wear` mesh, so rescaling its UVs would
break the body; on Seed-san `eye` is the only sane proxy for "the head material". On it Whale-chan's
texture is of course scrambled (the Seed-san eye UV layout != her face crop) - the inspection quad in
the harness shows the whole cell, the head shows the offsets applied to real geometry.

### What the mouth blob actually was (three causes, none of them the one VERIFY.md guessed)

Render of the ORIGINAL Seed-san head: thin closed smile, thin brows (`evidence/head_original_seedsan_000_head.png`).
Rendered the pipeline head after each fix:

1. **Alpha mode lost twice** - real, but not the blob. `eye_trans` (the mouth/brow overlay plane) is
   `alphaMode BLEND` in Seed-san. `make_stock_input.py` -> add-on `convert_mtoon1_to_bsdf_principled`
   copies only the constant alpha factor, so the glTF exporter wrote OPAQUE (the old stock GLB had
   `eye_trans OPAQUE`; `glass` survived only because its constant alpha < 1). Then `build_vrm.py` ->
   add-on `convert_material_to_mtoon1` never sets the MToon alpha mode, so even `glass` came out
   OPAQUE in the built VRM. Fixed: `make_stock_input.restore_alpha_mode` wires the texture alpha
   (BLEND) or the importer chain `1 - (a < cutoff)` (MASK) into the Principled BSDF and sets
   `surface_render_method`; `build_vrm.detect_alpha_mode` reads the imported node tree
   (`surface_render_method`, linked Alpha socket, clip chain / cutoff, constant alpha < 1) and applies
   `gltf.alpha_mode` / `alpha_cutoff` after conversion. Manifest of the rebuilt VRM: `eye_trans BLEND`,
   `glass BLEND` (were OPAQUE). Render after this fix alone: blob unchanged.
2. **MToon outline on the face-parts material** - the heavy brows. The pipeline gave every material a
   2.5 mm inverted-hull outline; the Seed-san `eye` / `eye_trans` / `robo_face` materials have
   `outlineWidthMode none`. Fixed: the face material (regex) and every BLEND material get
   `outline_width_mode = none`. Render: brows thin again, blob still there.
3. **Skin outline through the lip slit** - the blob. It is the `body_bake` outline shell poking through
   the modelled mouth slit (blob colour = the body_bake outline tint). At 1.2 mm it shrinks to a dark
   bracket around the lips (`evidence/head_outline12mm_residual_000_head.png`); Seed-san itself ships
   `outlinemask_body` as `outlineWidthMultiplyTexture` to zero the outline there, which a plain GLB
   cannot carry and an auto-rigger will not produce. Fixed for the proof with `--outline-skip
   "^body_bake$"` (new option); default width lowered to 1.2 mm. Render
   (`evidence/head_fixed_000_head.png` / `_happy_` / `_blink_`): thin closed smile, thin brows, neutral
   yellow irises / happy pink irises / blink closed lids - matches the original. For Whale-chan this
   residual does not apply: her mouth is painted in the face texture, the head has no lip slit.

Also fixed on the way: the add-on copies the Principled "Base Color" socket (the Blender 0.8 grey
default when a texture is linked) as the MToon base-colour factor, so every textured material was 20 %
darker than the input; `build_vrm.py` now forces the factor to 1 when a texture is wired in.

Not the cause: premultiplied alpha (three.js glTF textures are straight; the Blender atlas bytes are
identical to the source: neutral cell vs `faceparts` mean/max diff 0 on all four channels, verified
with numpy) and the UV rescale (the rescaled eye-primitive UVs land on the identical pixels;
geometry / morph targets / default weights of the three files are byte-equal, verified).

## 3. three-vrm proof (verified in the Electron pet window)

`cd apps/desktop && npx electron ../../spikes/vrm/face_check_main.js --capture` (exit 0, 16 shots).
The page loads `out/build/stock_whalechan_face.vrm`, finds the `eye` material (`MToonMaterial`,
`isMToonMaterial true`, map 4096x4096, `flipY false`), frames the head and puts an inspection quad
under it that uses the SAME material object with UVs = the neutral cell, so whatever offset the
expression manager writes into the material is visible as a whole cell. `report.json` records the
weights and `material.map.offset` per shot:

| shot | weights | map.offset | what the screenshot shows (viewed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| expr_neutral | {} | (0, 0) | quad: her neutral face (open eyes, closed smile); head: thin Seed-san smile (alpha fix) with her texture fragments on the eye polys |
| expr_happy | happy 1 | (0.7998, 0.1665) | quad: happy cell (closed happy eyes, smile, blush); head eye polys turn pink/blush |
| expr_blink | blink 1 | (0.19995, 0) | quad: closed eyes cell; head: Seed-san lids also closed by the stock morph bind |
| expr_aa | aa 1 | (0.7998, 0) | quad: open `aa` mouth |
| expr_surprised | surprised 1 | (0.59985, 0.33301) | quad: surprised cell |
| xfade_binary_50 | happy 0.5, surprised 0.5 | (0, 0) | neutral - both binds are isBinary, `outputWeight` is 0 at or below 0.5 |
| xfade_binary_60 | happy 0.4, surprised 0.6 | (0.59985, 0.33301) | snaps to surprised |
| xfade_overlay_50_neutral_happy | {} + overlay 0.5 | (0, 0) | quad: a real 50 % blend (open eyes ghosted over the happy arcs, half-strength blush); head: same on the eye polys |

So `textureTransformBind` on MToon works in three-vrm 3.5.5 (it clones `map` and
`shadeMultiplyTexture` and adds the offset). Two things had to change in the export for it to be
usable, both found only by running it:

- **The first export's blink and visemes did nothing in three-vrm** (offset stayed (0, 0) at weight 1)
  because `apply_face_atlas` set `overrideBlink = block` on `blink` and `overrideMouth = block` on the
  visemes. three-vrm sums the overrides of ALL expressions and multiplies the blink-type / mouth-type
  / lookAt-type expression weights by `1 - sum`, so an expression that names its own category
  blocks itself. Corrected: every state blocks the other categories but never its own
  (blink: mouth + lookAt; visemes: blink + lookAt; looks: blink + mouth; everything else: all
  three). This also solves the additive-offset problem of one-material atlases: with a mood at
  weight 1, auto-blink and lip-sync are multiplied to 0 instead of adding a second offset and landing
  on a garbage cell. Cost: no blink / lip flap while a texture mood is at 1 - the runtime should
  either drop to neutral briefly to blink, or use the eyes-closed variants (blink cell) as the blink
  of that mood.
- **isBinary crossfade is a snap**, as the README said: at exactly 0.5/0.5 both are off (a one-frame
  neutral flash if the runtime lerps through it); at 0.6 the target snaps in. `face_check.html`
  implements the alternative the README described - `lab.overlay(from, to, t)` clones the face
  SkinnedMesh with a cloned MToon material sampling the target cell (`transparent`, `opacity = t`,
  `depthWrite false`, polygon offset) - and the 50 % shot is a real blend. Note for the runtime: a
  material that is not in `vrm.materials` must have its `update(dt)` called by hand or its UV
  transform / opacity uniforms never upload (the first overlay capture was invisible for that reason).

Not done: no `gltf-validator` run in this session (none installed here); the validator of the add-on
ran clean inside each build and both VRMs re-import in Blender (`head_fixed_reimport_check.json`:
spec 1.0, 72 bones, 51 humanoid, 4 springs) and load in three-vrm.

## What the owner GLB still needs (likely)

- The face material must be its own material with the face island unwrapped to the face crop:
  `face_atlas_states.json.texture.face_in_cell_uv` = `[0.0195, 0.0117, 0.9597, 0.9765]` of the cell
  (`build_vrm.py` maps mesh UV 0..1 to the whole cell). Everything outside the crop is skin colour.
- Every non-neutral mood is a whole-face cell, so eyes and mouth cannot animate independently on top
  of a mood; that is the price of the single texture-transform path and is why the per-region overlay
  shader remains the better long-term design (VERIFY.md gap 1, option b).
- 4096x4096 RGBA is 64 MB of GPU texture; `--size 2048` halves the cell to 409x341 (eye 122 px wide) if
  that matters on the target machines.
