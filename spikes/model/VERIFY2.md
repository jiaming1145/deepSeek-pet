## The one question: is there ONE documented command from `spikes/model/generated/<service>/*.glb` to "her VRM with her face and tail physics, moving in the window"?

**No — it is two commands, one of which has no documented path to the window, plus two known defects on the way.**

What exists and works (proven on the stock GLB in this session):
1. `python tools\vrm\hookup_whalechan.py --glb spikes\model\generated\<service>\<file>.glb` -> `tools/vrm/out/whalechan/<run>/whalechan.vrm` with her face texture (30 texture-transform expressions), tail/ear/hair/skirt spring chains, MToon, manifest, Blender renders and the compare sheet. Documented in `tools/vrm/REPORT_hookup.md` and `CHECKLIST_incoming_glb.md`.
2. The VRM loads and animates in three-vrm (`spikes/vrm/actions/lab.html?vrm=<url>` accepts a model; `face_check_main.js --vrm <relative path> --mat <regex>` accepts one too).

What is missing (exact list):
- **No harness command loads the hookup output.** `spikes/vrm/index.html` hardcodes `./models/VRM1_Constraint_Twist_Sample.vrm` (line 65) and `spikes/vrm/main.js` has no `--vrm`; `spikes/vrm/actions/main.js` does not forward a `--vrm` flag even though `lab.js` reads `?vrm=`. The only mention is one line in `actions/README.md` ("`lab.html?vrm=<url>` loads another model") with no runnable command. Needed: `--vrm <path>` in `spikes/vrm/main.js` and `actions/main.js` (passed as a `loadFile` query, relative or absolute), and a README line. Note: passing a `file:///` URL as an argv value to `npx electron` aborts before the script runs (Electron treats it as an app URL; exit 127 / -1 with no output) — use a relative path.
- **`hookup_whalechan.py` does not chain into the window.** It stops at Blender renders; the "shows it moving" half needs either a final step that runs the Electron capture on the produced VRM or a documented second command. `docs/3D_PIPELINE.md` must name `hookup_whalechan.py` (not raw `build_vrm.py` with the fraction file) as the command.
- **Face defect A (hookup route):** the mouth outline bracket returns because the hookup neither passes `--outline-skip` nor scales `--outline-width` with `--height`. Fix: scale the outline width by the applied height factor in `build_vrm.py` (or have hookup pass `--outline-width 0.0012*scale`), and let `chains.whalechan.json` / hookup carry an `outline_skip` regex for the skin material.
- **Face defect B:** `apply_face_atlas` must clear or replace the face material's emissive (and any matcap/rim) textures; otherwise a face material with a secondary map washes cells white under the UV offset.
- **Face material selection is a guess** (`face|skin_face|head`, first match). On the stock it landed on a backpack texture. The owner's GLB needs its face material identified from the probe output before the face step is trusted, and its face island unwrapped into the neutral cell (`face_in_cell_uv`) — the hookup does not do the unwrap.
- **Not a blocker but unproven:** tail physics is proven only as spring joints moving on the Seed-san tail slab in three-vrm (numbers, not a visible flick in the 640x900 frame); chibi spring values are untuned; `--force-tpose` remains untested on a real A-pose export (skips silently on shape keys, so a Meshy rig with blendshapes will export A-pose).
