# Cubism checklist

1. Open the import PSD as a new model and confirm every expected layer becomes an ArtMesh.
2. Verify registration, group hierarchy, and draw order.
3. Run automatic mesh generation on broad, clean parts only.
4. Manually inspect lashes, eyelids, mouth lines, thin hair, ribbons, fingers, and irregular accessories.
5. Create or review face deformers before facial motion generation.
6. Test head angle, blink, eye tracking, mouth open/form, body angle, and breathing before physics.
7. Configure clipping and draw-order changes.
8. Add physics only after base deformation is stable.
9. Revisit source art through PSD re-import when a seam or hidden-region defect appears; do not paint around structural errors inside Cubism.
10. Export runtime files only after a final movement and texture-atlas review.
