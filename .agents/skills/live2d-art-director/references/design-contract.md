# Canonical design contract

The canonical image must make separation and rigging easier, not merely look attractive.

Required:

- front view with minimal perspective distortion;
- neutral head and torso angle;
- both eyes fully readable;
- neutral mouth and visible jaw contour;
- hair construction that can be divided into back mass, bangs, side locks, and physics strands;
- arms and hands not fused into torso or accessories;
- clear garment boundaries and joint coverage;
- plain or transparent background;
- consistent cel-style light direction.

Record exact invariants for face shape, eye spacing, iris design, hairline, palette, costume geometry, accessories, and intentional asymmetry. Every later edit prompt must say what may change and that all other canonical pixels and identity traits must remain unchanged.
